# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-microscope" %}Research

Our mission is to shape the future of computing and cyber-physical systems, making them increasingly efficient, secure, and fully autonomous.

In practice, our research comes to life across three main areas:

- Mobile autonomous systems: drones, smart vehicles, and robotics.

- Critical infrastructures: advanced monitoring of buildings, road networks, and energy systems.

- High-Performance Computing (HPC): large-scale, ultra-high-performance computing architectures.

### How we work
We don't just study individual components. We adopt a holistic approach that bridges hardware architecture, software layers, and end applications. We develop and optimize cyber-physical, neuromorphic, and HPC technologies across the entire spectrum: from embedded and Edge devices to Cloud infrastructures.

### Our goal
We want to drive the entire data chain—from the sensor to the supercomputer—at peak performance. All while minimizing energy and thermal consumption, and ensuring robust cybersecurity at every stage of the process.
