Filippo Samorì is a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical, Electronic, and Information Engineering “Guglielmo Marconi” at the University of Bologna, where he also received his Master’s degree with honors in Automation Engineering. His research focuses on developing neuromorphic algorithms, particularly Spiking Neural Networks, for resource-constrained embedded systems and robotic applications such as Nano-UAVs.
