# Internship opportunities

**Step out of the classroom and into the lab.** 
Our internships offer a hands-on dive into real-world challenges. You will work side-by-side with our researchers on cutting-edge projects involving drones, smart infrastructures, and edge computing. It’s the perfect launchpad for your career in tech and research, giving you the chance to write code and design systems that actually run in the real world.

*Interested? Send us your CV and a brief message explaining what drives your passion for tech!*

# Thesis opportunities

**Looking for a thesis topic that actually matters?** 
We offer challenging and highly rewarding thesis projects tailored to your interests and skills. When you write your thesis with us, you won't just be producing a document for the archives; you will be actively contributing to ongoing, cutting-edge research. 

## Bachelor thesis

**Start your research journey.** 
A Bachelor thesis with us is your chance to get your hands dirty with applied technologies. You will tackle specific, well-defined problems in mobile autonomous systems, cybersecurity, or embedded devices. It is a fantastic way to build a solid foundation and see how theoretical concepts are applied to modern hardware.

## Master thesis

**Dive deep and innovate.** 
For your Master thesis, we expect you to push the envelope. You will take ownership of complex challenges in neuromorphic engineering, HPC optimization, or holistic system design. We treat our Master students as junior researchers: we expect critical thinking, creativity, and dedication.

# Phd opportunities

**Become an expert and lead the change.** 
A PhD with our group means years of intense, rewarding, and high-impact research. You will lead your own core projects, collaborate with international academic and industrial partners, and tackle the most pressing challenges in energy-efficient architectures, cybersecurity, and autonomous systems. 

**Open Positions:** We periodically open fully-funded PhD positions. However, if you have a stellar academic background, a bold research idea, and the drive to pursue it, don't wait for an official call, reach out to us directly to discuss potential sponsorships and future openings!
